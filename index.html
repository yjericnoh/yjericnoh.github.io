About

I am an undergraduate in Mechanical Engineering at UNIST. My research interests are contact and friction modeling of continuum and soft robots, soft-robot control, and robot manipulation and learning from demonstration.

Since December 2025 I have been an undergraduate researcher in the Intelligent Robotics Lab (IntRo) at UNIST, advised by Prof. Junhyoung Ha, where I lead a project on law-preserving resolution selection for contact-accurate continuum-robot simulation.

From February to May 2027 I will be an exchange student in the School of Engineering at HKUST.

Research

Dec 2025 –

Law-preserving resolution selection for contact-accurate continuum-robot simulation

Intelligent Robotics Lab (IntRo), UNIST · Undergraduate researcher, project lead · Advisor: Prof. Junhyoung Ha

  • Showed in pilot runs that refining a MuJoCo rigid-chain model of a tendon-driven continuum robot can change its contact law: per-length contact stiffness rose by about 95–108% from 10 to 80 links once link rotational inertia mattered.
  • Developed a law-preserving parameterization that scales each contact row by the engine's inverse-inertia estimate; it held the target per-row stiffness to machine precision in the same pilots.
  • Designing an output-oriented resolution-selection method (adjoint-based estimates of wrench and squeeze-force changes) checked against an independent rod-model reference, toward a simulation paper.

MuJoCo · Python · tendon-driven continuum robots · adjoint-based error estimation

May – Sep 2026

Teleoperation feedback for imitation-learning data collection

UNIST UIRP team project

  • Leader–follower teleoperation on OpenManipulator-X (ROS 2 Jazzy, ros2_control, Dynamixel), comparing six feedback conditions — from unilateral control to variable stiffness with tactile feedback — as data-collection methods for imitation learning.
  • Implemented MuJoCo-based gripper force feedback from simulated contact forces; set up the environment and data pipeline, ran calibration, and improved the tactile tokenizer.
  • Trained manipulation policies on frozen DINOv2 visual features with action chunking and evaluated them closed-loop in MuJoCo.

ROS 2 Jazzy · ros2_control · Dynamixel · MuJoCo · PyTorch · DINOv2 · OpenManipulator-X
